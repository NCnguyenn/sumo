## D-Pad

Create ONE polished high-fidelity Android mobile UI design mockup for a Vietnamese Sumo robot controller, not a photograph. This is a completely new visual design proposal. The ENTIRE image is one flat, front-facing, edge-to-edge LANDSCAPE smartphone screen, approximately 20:9 aspect ratio. No device bezel, no hands, no perspective, no surrounding poster or desktop. Large clear legible Vietnamese UI lettering. Premium practical mobile gamepad design, designed for two thumbs, not a desktop dashboard.

APP PALETTE: background deep charcoal navy #0B1220; control panels #172338; thin borders #2B3B53; main text #F4F7FB; secondary text #A8B5C8; navigation and connected status teal #2DD4BF. KILL emergency red #F04452. BOOST pink red #FF647C. VŨ KHÍ warm orange #F6AD55. QUAY NHANH soft yellow #F6D365. BRAKE blue #60A5FA. Use colored outlines and subdued tinted dark button backgrounds, with restrained filled highlights. White labels on dark surfaces; dark lettering on bright filled surfaces. Matte modern surfaces, softly rounded corners, extremely subtle depth, crisp simple line icons. No gratuitous neon glow, neon cyberpunk, decorative gears, illustrations, charts or tiny technical clutter.

LAYOUT, extremely important:
1. A slim but touch-friendly HEADER approximately 14% of screen height. Left aligned small SUMO wordmark, next a teal dot and the exact text "Đã kết nối" and small "12 ms", with a subdued "MANUAL" pill. Toward right are clearly tappable outlined "Cấu hình" with gear icon, then "Cập nhật" with download icon. Far top right is a distinct solid red emergency button labeled "KILL" with stop icon. KILL must be easy to spot, separated by spacing from other controls.
2. MAIN BODY approximately 70% of screen height, TWO columns only. Left column 45% of available width: a spacious panel containing a large four-way D-pad suited for the LEFT THUMB, with four very large rounded buttons arranged as a cross. Up button arrow + "TIẾN", left arrow + "TRÁI", right arrow + "PHẢI", down arrow + "LÙI". Center is a small neutral indicator, NOT a new stop button. The four hit zones are large and distinct. Panel has discreet label "ĐIỀU HƯỚNG" and small "D-PAD" badge. No joystick in this screen.
Right column 55% of available width: FOUR large equally balanced rounded action buttons in a 2 by 2 grid, suited for the RIGHT THUMB. TOP LEFT pink-red lightning icon and "BOOST", sublabel "GIỮ ĐỂ TĂNG TỐC". TOP RIGHT orange weapon icon and "VŨ KHÍ", sublabel "GIỮ · HOLD". BOTTOM LEFT yellow rotation arrow and "QUAY NHANH", sublabel "200 ms". BOTTOM RIGHT blue brake icon and "BRAKE", sublabel "GIỮ PHANH". Use generous breathing room between buttons. BOOST must NOT occupy half the screen or dwarf the others.
3. FOOTER approximately 13% screen height: one low horizontal panel. Left label "GIỚI HẠN TỐC" and bold "60%"; wide teal slider with a thick touch-friendly thumb. At right compact readouts "PWM TRÁI 0" and "PWM PHẢI 0", plus small "30 ms". No tall center column of telemetry. Small subdued lock icon and "AUTO khóa" can appear near manual status without looking tappable.

All primary labels large enough to read on an actual phone. Keep all controls inside safe margins. Aligned grid, consistent spacing. UI should feel attractive, calm, robust and immediately understandable. Exact functional control set only; do not add navigation arrows, account avatars, battery meters, extra STOP buttons, weapon illustrations or fake telemetry. This is a design mockup; connected status and numbers are illustrative. Output ONLY the finished app screen, no explanatory prose or annotations outside it.
